Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

BloodLink Blog is an educational website that publishes information about blood donation. This page explains the basic data practices for this website.

Information you provide

This static website does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information. If you use the Report a problem email link, your email application opens and any information you send is handled by your email provider and the recipient.

Analytics

When Google Analytics 4 is configured with the website owner's Measurement ID, it may collect website usage information such as pages visited, approximate device/browser details, and interactions. Analytics is not active until the placeholder Measurement ID in the website files is replaced with a valid ID. Google's own privacy terms apply to its processing.

Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies after it is configured. Browser settings can be used to control cookies. Applicable consent requirements depend on the locations of the website owner and visitors.

External links and images

Articles may link to external websites or load images from third-party image hosts. Those services may process connection data according to their own policies. For better control, host images locally in the assets folder.

Children and sensitive information

Please do not send sensitive medical information through the website. BloodLink Blog is not a medical service and does not provide emergency care.

Contact

For privacy questions, replace this sentence with your official BloodLink support email before publishing.
